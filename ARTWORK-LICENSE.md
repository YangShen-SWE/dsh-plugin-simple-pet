## v0.2.6 sources / 新素材来源

Four characters were generated with GPT Image 2 for this project: Snow Whale
(`snow`), Mint Tea (`mint`), Cherry Soda (`cherry`), and Star-Sand Mage (`star`).
Their source sheets are retained byte-for-byte with the embedded AI-generation
provenance. Runtime atlases are cropped, cleaned, scaled and aligned derivatives;
their PNG text identifies the original source and its SHA-256. A source signature
is NOT copied onto edited pixels. Retention is not a claim that signatures or the
certificate chain were independently verified.

裁切、清理和对齐，在 PNG 文本中注明源图及 SHA-256，未把原图签名冒用到修改后
像素上。保留来源信息不代表已经独立验证签名或证书链。
